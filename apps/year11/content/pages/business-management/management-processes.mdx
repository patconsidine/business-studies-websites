The interdependence of the different key business functions – marketing, operations, finance and human resources must be considered by managers when making decisions. Therefore, managers must understand how their decisions will impact all areas of the business. Each function completes different types of tasks for a business to ensure business goals are achieved . All business functions need to work together to ensure products are produced and delivered in the same way.

### The main business functions are

Operations focus on strategies to improve production processes and to create the ideal factory or office layout Marketing determine the appropriate markets for the business’s products, and decide on pricing, product features, promotion and channels of distribution Finance is responsible for the financial requirements, budget allocation and financial record keeping Human Resources is concerned with the recruiting, training, employment contracts and separation of the employees who are required to run the business successfully
