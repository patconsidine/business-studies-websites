## Why operations matter

Operations management is the process of planning, organizing and controlling the production process so a business can meet customer expectations.

## Core ideas

- Inputs, transformation process and outputs
- Efficiency and productivity measures
- Quality management and continuous improvement

## Case study spotlight

Pick one Australian business and identify two operations strategies it uses to improve quality or efficiency.
