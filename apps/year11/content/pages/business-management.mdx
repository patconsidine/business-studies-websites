<ReadingSection title="What is management?">

**Traditional view:** management is coordinating a business's **limited resources** to achieve its goals.

**Contemporary view:** working **with and through other people** to achieve goals in a **constantly changing environment** — using resources **effectively and efficiently**.

Managers draw on **communication, interpersonal, strategic and organisational skills** to lead diverse teams with different responsibilities.

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="POLC — four management functions">

<LessonImage src="/images/diagrams/polc-cycle.svg" alt="Circular diagram of the four management functions: Planning, Organising, Leading and Controlling" caption="POLC — the four functions of management" variant="diagram" />

- **Planning** — developing a predetermined course of action to meet business goals  
- **Organising** — structuring the organisation to achieve desired goals  
- **Leading** — influencing and motivating people to achieve goals  
- **Controlling** — comparing planned results with actual results and taking corrective action  

</WorkbookSection>
