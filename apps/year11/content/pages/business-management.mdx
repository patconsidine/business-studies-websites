The focus of this topic is the **nature and responsibilities of management** in the business environment.

## Students learn to

**Examine contemporary business issues to:**

- Discuss strategies that could reconcile the conflicting interests of stakeholders
- Compare and contrast approaches to management
- Explain the benefits of quality management practices

**Investigate aspects of business using case studies to:**

- Identify qualities of managers who have exhibited high personal and ethical standards
- Analyse different ways of coordinating key business functions for an SME
- Examine effective cash flow management
- Assess the role of the income statement and balance sheet when describing financial performance
- Explain how SMEs manage change effectively

## Study in this section

Work through each page below in order (or as directed in class):

1. **Nature of Management** — skills, POLC, traditional vs contemporary management
2. **Management Approaches** — classical (scientific, bureaucratic), behavioural, contingency
3. **Management Processes** — interdependence of business functions
4. **Operations Y11** · **Marketing Y11** · **Human Resources Y11** · **Finance Y11**
5. **Management & Change** — change, SMART goals, resistance, business information systems
