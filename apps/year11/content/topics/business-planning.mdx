## Syllabus focus

The processes of establishing and planning a small to medium enterprise.

## Why this topic matters

This topic culminates in preparing a **small business plan** (hypothetical or actual) in report format. Students integrate ideas from Topics 1 and 2 into one coherent plan.

## Small to medium enterprises (SMEs)

- **Definition**, **role** and **economic contribution**  
- **Success and failure** — know common influences on both  

### Influences in establishing an SME

| Area | What to consider |
|------|------------------|
| **Personal qualities** | Qualifications, skills, motivation, entrepreneurship, cultural background, gender |
| **Information sources** | Research and advice before starting |
| **Business idea** | Competition in the market |
| **Establishment options** | New business, existing business, franchise |
| **Market** | Goods/services, price, location |
| **Finance** | Source and cost of funds |
| **Legal** | Business name, zoning, health and other regulations |
| **Human resources** | Skills; wage and non-wage costs |
| **Taxation** | Federal/state taxes, local rates and charges |

## The business planning process

1. **Sources of planning ideas** and situational analysis  
2. **Vision**, goals and/or objectives (including long-term growth)  
3. **Organising resources** across operations, marketing, finance, HR  
4. **Forecasting** — total revenue, total cost, **break-even analysis**, **cash flow projections**  
5. **Monitoring and evaluation** — sales, budgets, profit, corrective action  

## Critical issues in success and failure

- Importance of a **business plan**  
- **Management** — staffing and teams  
- **Trend analysis**  
- Identifying and sustaining **competitive advantage**  
- Avoiding **over-extension** of finance and other resources  
- Using **technology**  
- **Economic conditions**  

## Students learn to (skills)

- Discuss **government influence** on SMEs  
- Assess effect of **two environmental changes** on SMEs  
- Explain how a business plan is determined in a real SME  
- Explain how SMEs enter the **global market** for long-term growth  
- Identify ways SMEs gain **competitive advantage**  
- **Prepare** a small business plan (hypothetical or actual) in business plan/report format  

## Business plan scaffold (report sections)

Use this structure in assessment tasks:

1. Executive summary  
2. Business description and legal structure  
3. Market analysis (target market, competition)  
4. Marketing plan (marketing mix)  
5. Operations plan  
6. Financial plan (forecasts, break-even, cash flow)  
7. HR plan  
8. Monitoring and evaluation  

## Practice prompt

For a hypothetical café or online store, outline **vision**, **two goals**, and one **break-even** insight (what costs and sales volume must be estimated?).  

## Exam tip

Link every section of a plan back to **P3** — what makes SMEs succeed or fail. Use realistic numbers in forecasts even in classroom tasks.
