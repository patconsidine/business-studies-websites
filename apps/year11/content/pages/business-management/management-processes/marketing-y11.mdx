<ReadingSection title="Topic focus">

**Marketing** is planning and executing the conception, pricing, promotion and distribution of a want-satisfying good, service or idea — and making it available to customers who want to buy it.

Marketing focuses on how the business satisfies consumer **needs and wants** through its products, guided by **market research**.

</ReadingSection>

</ActivitySection>

<ReadingSection title="Target markets and segmentation">

A **target market** is the group of customers a business intends to sell to, identified through **market segmentation** — dividing the total market by demographics, behaviour, location, psychographics or geographics.

| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Mass market** | One product mass produced, distributed and promoted to the entire market |
| **Market segmentation** | Subgroups sharing common characteristics; tailored strategies |
| **Niche market** | Narrowly selected segment (e.g. vegetarian-only café) — must confirm sufficient demand |

Segmentation allows efficient use of marketing resources, better understanding of buying behaviour and refined strategies over time.

</ReadingSection>

<ReadingSection title="Competitive advantage in marketing">

**Competitive advantage** — the edge a business has over rivals in the target market's perception.

Strategies that are hard for competitors to copy:

| Factor | How it creates advantage |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **Value** | Quality and size relative to price |
| **Benefits** | Tangible and intangible; **brand image** often most important |
| **Price** | Low prices to gain share when new; **prestige pricing** when established |

</ReadingSection>

<ReadingSection title="The marketing mix (4 Ps)">

The business controls these elements to reach its target market:

| P | Focus |
|---|--------|
| **Product** | Good or service — tangibles (product, packaging) and intangibles (service, branding, positioning) |
| **Price** | Compatible with marketing and finance objectives — cost-based, market-based, competition-based, prestige |
| **Promotion** | Inform, persuade, remind — advertising, sales promotion, direct selling, publicity, product placement, social media |
| **Place** | Distribution — transport, warehousing, outlets, degree of distribution (intensive, selective, exclusive) |

The emphasis on each P depends on **product positioning** and **product life cycle** stage.

</ReadingSection>
