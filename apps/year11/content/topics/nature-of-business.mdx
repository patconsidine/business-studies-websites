## Syllabus focus

The role and nature of business in a changing business environment.

## Why this topic matters

Every other Preliminary topic assumes students understand **what a business is**, **who it serves**, and **how external forces shape decisions**. This is your foundation for case studies, short answers and the business planning task later in the course.

## Students learn about

### Role of business

- **Nature of a business** — producing goods and services
- **Contributions** — profit, employment, incomes, choice, innovation, entrepreneurship and risk, wealth and quality of life

### Types of businesses

| Dimension | Examples to know |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Size** | SMEs vs large businesses |
| **Scope** | Local, national, global |
| **Industry** | Primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary, quinary |
| **Legal structure** | Sole trader, partnership, private company, public company, government enterprise |

**Choice of legal structure** depends on size, ownership and finance needs.

### Influences in the business environment

- **External** — economic, financial, geographic, social, legal, political, institutional, technological, competitive situation, markets
- **Internal** — products, location, resources, management and business culture

### Stakeholders

Identify **internal** and **external** stakeholders and explain how their interests can align or conflict.

### Business growth and decline

**Business life cycle stages:**

1. Establishment  
2. Growth  
3. Maturity  
4. Post-maturity  

For each stage, know **strategies** businesses use and **challenges** they face.

**Decline and cessation:**

- Factors contributing to decline  
- Voluntary vs involuntary cessation (including **liquidation**)  
- Problems for stakeholders when companies fail  

## Students learn to (skills)

- Discuss **global expansion** of an Australian business  
- Discuss **entry into Australia** by a global business  
- Explain how external influences contributed to growth of **tertiary, quaternary and quinary** industries in Australia  
- Identify stakeholder problems when companies go into **liquidation**  
- Distinguish business **types** and place real businesses on the **life cycle**  

## Key vocabulary

- SME, stakeholder, liquidation, legal structure, life cycle, quaternary/quinary industries  
- Internal vs external influences  

## Case study spotlight

Choose one Australian business expanding overseas (or one global brand entering Australia). In 5 bullet points, link:

- type of business (size, industry, legal structure)  
- one external influence  
- one stakeholder impact  

## Practice prompt

**Explain** how changes in **two external influences** could affect a small retailer at the **growth** stage of the business life cycle. (4–6 sentences, use business terminology.)

## Exam tip

Preliminary responses should **define**, **explain** and **apply** to a named business. Avoid lists without linking back to the question.
