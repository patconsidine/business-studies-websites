<ReadingSection title="Response types">

<LessonImage src="/images/diagrams/exam-response-types.svg" alt="Four exam response types: Multiple Choice, Short Answer, Extended Response and Business Report" caption="Preliminary exam response types and typical mark ranges" variant="diagram" />

Each question type needs a different approach:

| Page | Marks typical | Focus |
|------|---------------|--------|
| [Multiple Choice](/exam-preparation/multiple-choice) | 1 mark each | Eliminate wrong options; watch for "best" answer |
| [Short Answer](/exam-preparation/short-answer) | 3–6 marks | Define → explain → apply with an example |
| [Extended Response](/exam-preparation/extended-response) | 8–20 marks | TEEL paragraphs; syllabus depth; case studies |
| [Business Report](/exam-preparation/business-report) | Variable | Plan format; executive summary; recommendations |

</ReadingSection>

<Callout title="Nature of Business — Students learn to">

**Examine contemporary business issues to:**

- Discuss the **global expansion** of one Australian business  
- Discuss the **expansion into Australia** of one global business  
- Explain how changes in **external influences** contributed to growth of **tertiary, quaternary and quinary** industries in Australia  
- Identify problems for **stakeholders** when companies go into **liquidation**  

**Investigate using case studies to:**

- Distinguish between **types of businesses**  
- Identify businesses at different **life cycle** stages  
- Outline **strategies** appropriate for each life cycle stage  

</Callout>

<Callout title="Business Planning — Students learn to">

**Examine contemporary business issues to:**

- Discuss the **influence of government** on SMEs  
- Assess the effect of **two changes** in the business environment on SMEs  

**Investigate using case studies to:**

- Explain how the **business plan** is determined in at least one SME  
- Explain how SMEs enter the **global market** for long-term growth  
- Identify ways SMEs gain a **competitive advantage**  
- Prepare a **small business plan** in report format  

</Callout>

<Callout title="Business Management — Students learn to">

**Examine contemporary business issues to:**

- Discuss strategies reconciling **conflicting stakeholder** interests  
- **Compare and contrast** approaches to management  
- Explain benefits of **quality management** practices  

**Investigate using case studies to:**

- Identify qualities of managers with high **personal and ethical standards**  
- Analyse coordination of key business functions for an **SME**  
- Examine effective **cash flow management**  
- Assess the **income statement** and **balance sheet**  
- Explain how SMEs manage **change** effectively  

</Callout>

<ActivitySection title="Build your own practice paper">

1. Pick **one dot point** from each topic above.  
2. Write a question for each (MC, short answer and extended).  
3. Swap with a partner and attempt each other's questions under timed conditions.  
4. Mark using syllabus keywords and textbook definitions.

</ActivitySection>
