## Structure

- **Introduction** — define key terms; outline what you will discuss  
- **Body** — TEEL paragraphs (Topic, Explain, Example, Link)  
- **Conclusion** — judgement / summary linked to the question  

## TEEL reminder

- **T**opic sentence — answers the question directly  
- **E**xplain — business theory and syllabus terms  
- **E**xample — Australian case study or hypothetical SME  
- **L**ink — back to the question  

## Example task (from Nature of Business)

*Explain how businesses contribute to our economic and social well-being. Use current Australian examples.*

Plan **two body paragraphs**: one economic (profit, employment, incomes), one social (quality of life, ethics).
