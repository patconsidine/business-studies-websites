## Structure

1. **Define** the key term(s) in the question  
2. **Explain** the concept (cause/effect or features)  
3. **Apply** to a named business or case study (even one sentence)  

## Command words

| Word | What to do |
|------|------------|
| **Identify** | Name or list |
| **Outline** | Brief summary of main points |
| **Explain** | Show cause and effect; make clear |
| **Describe** | Characteristics or features |

## Exam tip

Match length to marks — roughly **2–3 lines per mark** unless otherwise stated.
