<ReadingSection title="Topic focus">

**Extended response** questions (typically **8–20 marks**) require **depth**, **structure** and **sustained analysis** — usually with **case study** or contemporary business examples.

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="TEEL paragraph structure">

| Part | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **T — Topic sentence** | States the main point of the paragraph |
| **E — Explanation** | Syllabus concept — define and explain how it works |
| **E — Example / evidence** | Real Australian business, case study or hypothetical |
| **L — Link** | Connect back to the question and lead to next point |

Use **one TEEL paragraph per main idea**. Higher-mark questions need **3–4 body paragraphs** plus introduction and conclusion.

</WorkbookSection>

<ReadingSection title="Essay structure (15–20 marks)">

**Introduction (2–3 sentences)**

- Define key terms from the question  
- Outline the **thesis** — what your response will argue or explain  
- Preview main points (optional for shorter responses)  

**Body (3–4 TEEL paragraphs)**

- Each paragraph = one syllabus theme or stakeholder / influence / function  
- Integrate **case study** detail — names, dates, strategies, outcomes  

**Conclusion (2–3 sentences)**

- Summarise main findings **without** introducing new content  
- Directly answer the question — "Therefore…" / "In conclusion…"  

</ReadingSection>

<ReadingSection title="Sample question — 15 marks">

**Question:** Explain how **internal and external influences** have affected the operations of a business you have studied. *(15 marks)*

**Suggested paragraph plan:**

1. **Intro** — Define internal vs external influences; name case study (e.g. General Motors, ALDI, a local SME).  
2. **Body 1 — External (economic/technological)** — TEEL on one external influence with example.  
3. **Body 2 — External (legal/social/competitive)** — TEEL on second external influence.  
4. **Body 3 — Internal (management/resources/culture)** — TEEL on internal response or adjustment.  
5. **Body 4 — Interdependence** — How influences combined; management's proactive response.  
6. **Conclusion** — Overall impact on operations; sustainability of strategy.  

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="Command word guide">

| Command | What examiners expect |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Explain** | Cause and effect; how and why |
| **Assess / Evaluate** | Strengths and limitations; judgement supported by evidence |
| **Discuss** | Range of perspectives; balanced analysis |
| **Compare and contrast** | Similarities **and** differences (e.g. classical vs behavioural management) |
| **Analyse** | Break into parts; show relationships |

</WorkbookSection>

<ActivitySection title="Practice extended responses">

Choose **one** question. Plan for 5 minutes, write for 25 minutes.

1. **Discuss** strategies that could reconcile the **conflicting interests of stakeholders** in an SME. *(15 marks)*  
2. **Compare and contrast** the **classical** and **behavioural** approaches to management. *(12 marks)*  
3. **Explain** how an SME can manage **change** effectively when facing new technology. *(15 marks)*  
4. **Assess** the role of the **income statement** and **balance sheet** when describing financial performance. *(15 marks)*  

Peer-mark using a syllabus checklist: definitions, examples, TEEL, conclusion.

</ActivitySection>
