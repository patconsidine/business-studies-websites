<ReadingSection title="Topic focus">

**Short answer** questions (typically **3–6 marks**) test whether you can **define**, **explain** and **apply** syllabus concepts — often with a **business example**.

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="The 3-step formula">

| Step | Marks | What to do |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Define** | 1–2 | State the syllabus definition in your own words |
| **Explain** | 1–2 | Show *how* or *why* the concept works |
| **Apply / example** | 1–2 | Link to a **real or hypothetical** Australian business |

**Command words:** *Outline* = brief points; *Explain* = cause and effect; *Describe* = characteristics; *Distinguish* = show differences between two things.

</WorkbookSection>

<ReadingSection title="Sample scaffold — 4 marks">

**Question:** Explain how **external economic influences** can affect a small business. *(4 marks)*

**Model response structure:**

1. **Define** — External economic influences are factors such as interest rates, inflation and unemployment over which the business has little control. *(1 mark)*  
2. **Explain** — When interest rates rise, the cost of **debt finance** increases, so an SME may delay purchasing equipment or expanding. In a **recession**, consumer spending falls, reducing sales revenue. *(2 marks)*  
3. **Apply** — For example, a local café may see fewer customers during an economic downturn as households cut discretionary spending. *(1 mark)*  

</ReadingSection>

<ReadingSection title="Sample scaffold — 5 marks">

**Question:** Outline **two** ways an SME can gain a **competitive advantage**. *(5 marks)*

**Model response structure:**

- **Point 1 + explanation** — Differentiation: offer a unique product or superior service competitors cannot easily copy (e.g. Bondi Sands — affordable Australian-made self-tan). *(2 marks)*  
- **Point 2 + explanation** — Cost leadership: achieve lower production costs through efficiency or economies of scale, allowing competitive pricing (e.g. ALDI's home-brand, low-overhead model). *(2 marks)*  
- **Link to syllabus** — Both strategies support long-term growth and market share. *(1 mark)*  

</ReadingSection>

<ActivitySection title="Practice questions">

Attempt under **5 minutes** each. Use define → explain → apply.

1. **Outline** the four management functions (POLC). *(4 marks)*  
2. **Distinguish** between fixed and variable costs. *(4 marks)*  
3. **Explain** one strategy businesses use to reconcile **conflicting stakeholder** interests. *(3 marks)*  
4. **Describe** two features of a **SMART** goal. *(4 marks)*  
5. **Outline** how **quality assurance** differs from **quality control**. *(5 marks)*  

Swap with a partner and mark using the syllabus.

</ActivitySection>

<WorkbookSection title="Sentence starters">

- "**X** refers to…"  
- "This means that…"  
- "For example, **business name** demonstrates this by…"  
- "As a result, the business…"  
- "One way / Another way…"  

</WorkbookSection>
