## Human resources role

Human resources supports business performance by managing people, culture and compliance.

## Focus areas

- Recruitment and selection
- Motivation and leadership
- Performance management and legal responsibilities

## Exam tip

Always link HR strategies to business outcomes such as productivity, staff retention and customer satisfaction.
