## Core focus

Finance strategies guide how businesses source funds, manage costs and improve profitability.

## Key areas

- Cash flow and working capital
- Profitability, liquidity and gearing ratios
- Short-term and long-term financial strategies

## Exam tip

Use at least one ratio result and explain what it means for business decision-making.
