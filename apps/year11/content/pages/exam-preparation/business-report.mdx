## When you see “business plan” or “report”

Present work in **business report format**:

- Title page / heading  
- Executive summary (if required)  
- Sections with clear headings  
- Formal tone; third person often appropriate  

## Typical business plan sections

1. Business description and legal structure  
2. Vision, goals and objectives  
3. Market analysis (target market, competition)  
4. Marketing plan (marketing mix)  
5. Operations plan  
6. Financial plan — forecasts, break-even, cash flow  
7. Human resources plan  
8. Monitoring and evaluation
