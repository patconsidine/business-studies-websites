## Syllabus focus

The nature and responsibilities of management in the business environment.

## Why this topic matters

This is the **largest** Preliminary topic. Students must connect **management approaches** to how **operations, marketing, finance and HR** work together in SMEs — and use basic financial statements in answers.

## Nature of management

### Features of effective management

- Interpersonal and communication skills  
- Strategic thinking, vision, problem-solving, decision-making  
- Flexibility and adaptability to change  
- Reconciling **conflicting stakeholder interests**  

### Achieving business goals

Managers balance:

- Profits, market share, growth, share price  
- **Social** and **environmental** goals  
- Often a **mix** of goals, not one alone  

**Staff involvement** — innovation, motivation, mentoring, training — supports these goals.

## Management approaches

| Approach | Key ideas | Leadership / structure |
|----------|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Classical** | Planning, organising, controlling | Hierarchical structure; autocratic leadership |
| **Behavioural** | Leading, motivating, communicating | Teams; participative/democratic leadership |
| **Contingency** | Adapting to circumstances | Flexible responses to internal/external change |

Be ready to **compare and contrast** approaches using a case study.

## Coordinating key business functions

### Operations

- Goods and/or services  
- Production process  
- **Quality management**  

### Marketing

- Target market identification  
- **Marketing mix** (product, price, promotion, place)  

### Finance

- **Cash flow statement**  
- **Income statement**  
- **Balance sheet**  

### Human resources

- Recruitment, training  
- Employment contracts  
- Separation (voluntary / involuntary)  

### Ethical business behaviour

Link ethics to decisions across all functions — not only HR.

## Management and change

- Responding to internal and external influences  
- Identifying need for change; setting achievable goals  
- **Resistance to change** and how SMEs manage change effectively  
- Role of **business information systems** and **management consultants**  

## Students learn to (skills)

- Discuss strategies to reconcile **conflicting stakeholder** interests  
- Compare **management approaches**  
- Explain benefits of **quality management**  
- Analyse coordination of functions for an **SME**  
- Examine **cash flow** management  
- Use income statement and balance sheet to describe **financial performance**  

## Practice prompt

**Analyse** how an SME could coordinate **marketing** and **finance** when launching a new product. Include one risk and one management approach that would help. (6–8 sentences)

## Exam tip

When a question names a function (e.g. marketing), still mention **interdependence** with at least one other function for stronger marks.
