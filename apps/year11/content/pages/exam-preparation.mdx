## Students learn to — practice scaffolds

Exam questions are often drawn from **Students learn to** dot points. Use these to write your own practice questions.

### Nature of Business

**Examine contemporary business issues to:**

- Discuss the global expansion of one Australian business
- Discuss the expansion into Australia of one global business
- Explain how changes in external influences contributed to growth of tertiary, quaternary and quinary industries in Australia
- Identify problems that arise for stakeholders when companies go into liquidation

**Investigate using case studies to:**

- Distinguish between different types of businesses
- Identify actual businesses at different stages of the business life cycle
- Outline possible business strategies appropriate for each life cycle stage

### Business Management

**Examine contemporary business issues to:**

- Discuss strategies to reconcile conflicting stakeholder interests
- Compare and contrast approaches to management
- Explain benefits of quality management practices

**Investigate using case studies to:**

- Identify qualities of ethical managers
- Analyse coordination of key business functions for an SME
- Examine effective cash flow management
- Assess income statement and balance sheet when describing financial performance
- Explain how SMEs manage change effectively

### Business Planning

**Examine contemporary business issues to:**

- Discuss the influence of government on SMEs
- Assess the effect of two changes in the business environment on SMEs

**Investigate using case studies to:**

- Explain how the business plan is determined in at least one SME
- Explain how SMEs can enter the global market for long-term growth
- Identify ways SMEs gain competitive advantage
