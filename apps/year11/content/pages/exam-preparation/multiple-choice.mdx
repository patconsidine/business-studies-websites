<ReadingSection title="Topic focus">

**Multiple choice** questions test syllabus knowledge quickly. Each question is usually worth **1 mark**. Read carefully — the best answer may not be the only *correct* one.

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="MC technique checklist">

1. **Read the stem first** — identify the command (which, what, best, most likely).  
2. **Cover options** — predict the answer before looking at A–D.  
3. **Eliminate** — cross out clearly wrong options.  
4. **Compare remaining** — choose the **most complete** or **syllabus-accurate** answer.  
5. **Watch traps** — "always", "never", "only" are often wrong; two options may look similar.  
6. **Manage time** — don't spend more than ~1 minute per question; flag and return.  

</WorkbookSection>

<ReadingSection title="Common question styles">

| Style | What to look for |
|-------|------------------|
| **Definition** | Exact syllabus wording for terms (SME, liquidation, SWOT) |
| **Best example** | Which scenario *best* illustrates the concept |
| **Cause and effect** | What happens when interest rates rise, or during recession |
| **Function match** | Which business function handles recruitment, break-even, etc. |
| **Management approach** | Classical vs behavioural vs contingency characteristics |

</ReadingSection>

<ActivitySection title="Practice — try these">

**1.** Which legal structure is most associated with shares traded on the ASX?

- A. Sole trader  
- B. Partnership  
- C. Public company  
- D. Franchise only  

**2.** Break-even analysis is most closely linked to which Preliminary topic?

- A. Nature of business only  
- B. Business planning  
- C. HSC Operations  
- D. Corporate liquidation  

**3.** The Hawthorne Effect is associated with which management approach?

- A. Classical scientific  
- B. Classical bureaucratic  
- C. Behavioural  
- D. None of the above  

**4.** Which is an **external** influence on a business?

- A. Business culture  
- B. Product mix  
- C. Inflation  
- D. Staffing levels  

**5.** Total Quality Management (TQM) means:

- A. Only the quality department checks finished goods  
- B. Everyone in the business is responsible for quality  
- C. Quality is inspected only at the end of production  
- D. Quality applies only to manufacturing  

*(Check answers with your teacher or textbook.)*
