<ReadingSection title="Topic focus">

The Preliminary syllabus requires students to **prepare a small business plan** presented in **business plan / report format**. This page scaffolds that structure — use it for assessment tasks and exam-style report questions.

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="Report sections">

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Title page** | Business name, student name, date, subject |
| **Executive summary** | One-page snapshot — goals, key strategies, financial highlight (write last) |
| **The business idea** | Gap in market, name, logo concept, goods/services, location, legal structure |
| **Situational analysis (SWOT)** | Internal strengths/weaknesses; external opportunities/threats |
| **Vision and goals** | Vision statement; SMART strategic, tactical and operational objectives |
| **Operational plan** | Inputs, processes, suppliers, equipment, staffing needs |
| **Marketing plan** | Target market, segmentation, 4 Ps (product, price, promotion, place) |
| **Financial plan** | Start-up costs, sales forecast, break-even, cash flow projection (12 months) |
| **Human resources plan** | Structure, roles, qualifications, training |
| **Monitoring and evaluation** | How sales, budget and profit will be tracked; corrective action |
| **Conclusion / future prospects** | Growth plans; summary of viability |
| **Appendices** | Optional — charts, logos, detailed budgets |

</WorkbookSection>

<ReadingSection title="Executive summary tips">

Write the executive summary **after** completing all other sections. Include:

- What the business does and who it serves  
- Key opportunity in the market  
- Top **three** goals for the first 12 months  
- Break-even target or funding required  
- Why the plan is viable  

Keep it to **one page** — a busy lender or teacher should grasp the whole plan quickly.

</ReadingSection>

<ReadingSection title="Financial plan essentials">

Include at minimum:

| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Set-up / start-up costs** | One-off costs before opening |
| **Operating costs** | Fixed and variable monthly costs |
| **Sales forecast** | Expected units × price for 12 months |
| **Break-even analysis** | FC ÷ (P − VC) — show working |
| **Cash flow projection** | Monthly inflows and outflows; identify shortfalls |

Link to [Business Planning](/business-planning) for formulas and worked examples.

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="Report writing conventions">

- Use **headings and subheadings** matching the sections above  
- Write in **third person** or **future tense** for plans ("The business will…")  
- Support claims with **data** — market research, cost estimates, competitor analysis  
- Use **tables and dot points** for SWOT, budgets and timelines  
- **Recommendations** should flow logically from the SWOT and financial analysis  

</WorkbookSection>

<ActivitySection title="Business report task">

Prepare a **small business plan** for a hypothetical or actual SME (café, online store, local service). Minimum requirements:

1. Complete **SWOT** with at least three points per quadrant  
2. Write **two SMART goals**  
3. Include a **break-even calculation** with assumptions stated  
4. Draft a **one-paragraph executive summary**  

Use the section checklist above. Submit in report format with a title page.

</ActivitySection>

<ActivitySection title="Peer review checklist">

Swap reports with a partner. Does the plan include:

- [ ] Clear business idea and target market?  
- [ ] SWOT linked to strategies?  
- [ ] Financial section with break-even?  
- [ ] HR and marketing aligned with operations?  
- [ ] Monitoring plan for goals?  

</ActivitySection>
