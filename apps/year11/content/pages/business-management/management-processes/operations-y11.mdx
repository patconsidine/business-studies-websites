<ReadingSection title="Topic focus">

**Operations management** — all activities managers engage in to produce a good or service. Operations creates, runs and controls a **transformation process** that converts inputs into outputs customers need.

</ReadingSection>

<ReadingSection title="Competitive position through operations">

Operations affects competitive position through:

- **Quality** of goods and services  
- **Cost** of production  
- **Availability** when customers demand the product  

</ReadingSection>

<ReadingSection title="The production process">

Three main elements: **inputs → transformation (process) → outputs**

**Inputs** — resources from the business or suppliers. Divided into:

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Transformed resources** | Materials, information, customers (when their choices shape inputs) |
| **Transforming resources** | Human resources (people), facilities (plant, machinery) |

**Manufactured goods** — physical processing of inputs into finished products.

**Services** — differ in three ways:

- Outputs cannot be held in stock  
- Heavy **customer interaction** shapes the output  
- More **labour intensive**  

</ReadingSection>

<WorkbookSection title="Operations definitions">

- **Operations management** — activities to produce goods or services through a transformation process  
- **Quality** — degree of excellence a product or service possesses  
- **Quality control** — inspections at points in production to check for defects  
- **Quality assurance** — proactive systems to achieve set production standards  
- **Total Quality Management (TQM)** — holistic approach; everyone responsible for quality  
- **Continuous improvement** — constant evaluation and raising of standards  

</WorkbookSection>

<ReadingSection title="Four approaches to quality">

| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Quality control** | Inspections at various production points |
| **Quality assurance** | Systems ensuring standards are met before defects occur |
| **TQM** | Whole-business, customer-focused, defect-free aim; employee empowerment |
| **Continuous improvement** | Ongoing evaluation — higher standards over time (e.g. Kaizen mindset) |
