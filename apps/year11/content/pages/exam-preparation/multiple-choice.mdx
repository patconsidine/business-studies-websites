## Tips

- Read the **stem** and all four options before choosing
- Eliminate clearly wrong options first
- Watch for **absolute** words (always, never) — often incorrect
- Link the question to **syllabus language** (e.g. life cycle stage, management approach)
- Use your [Kahoot revision](/exam-preparation) for timed practice

## Practice

Use past Preliminary papers and class quizzes. When reviewing mistakes, write the **correct syllabus term** in your workbook (grey section).
