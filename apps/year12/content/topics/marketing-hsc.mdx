## Strategic marketing lens

At Year 12 level, marketing responses should show judgment about strategy effectiveness, not just definition.

## Priority areas

- Situational analysis and SMART objectives
- Target market strategy and marketing mix alignment
- Ethical and legal influences on strategy choices

## Exam technique

When asked to evaluate, balance strengths and weaknesses before reaching a justified final judgment.
